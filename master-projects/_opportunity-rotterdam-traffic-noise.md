    <h4 id="rotterdam-traffic-noise">Using data to tackle noise pollution from antisocial driving in Rotterdam</h4>
    <p>
      Traffic noise can disturb sleep and reduce quality of life. In Rotterdam, loud incidents from antisocial driving are a particular concern. The municipality is working to better understand and tackle this nuisance through noise monitoring and targeted measures.
    </p>
    <p>
      In this MSc project, you will help Rotterdam develop a data-informed approach to tackling traffic noise. Choose your thesis direction from several research angles: analyse existing sensor data alongside complaint records to investigate which noise patterns lead to complaints, or explore where Rotterdam should measure noise to better capture residents' exposure. There may also be opportunities for hands-on measurements using noise sensors developed at TU Delft. You can shape the concrete research direction directly with Rotterdam, based on your interests and the municipality's needs.
    </p>
    <p><strong>Supervision:</strong> Lion Cassens (TPM, TU Delft)<br>
       <strong>In collaboration with:</strong> Municipality of Rotterdam</p>
