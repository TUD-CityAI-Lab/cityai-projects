    <h4 id="rotterdam-traffic-noise">Using data to tackle noise pollution from antisocial driving in Rotterdam</h4>
    <p>
      Traffic noise can disturb sleep and reduce quality of life. In Rotterdam, noise from antisocial driving is a particular concern. The municipality is working to better understand and tackle this nuisance through noise monitoring, noise cameras, and targeted measures.
    </p>
    <p>
      With this thesis project, you can help Rotterdam in a data-informed approach to tackling traffic noise. Choose your thesis direction from several research angles: analyse existing sensor data alongside complaint records to investigate which noise patterns lead to complaints, or explore where noise sensors should be placed to better capture residents' exposure. You may also use AI-powered noise sensors developed by our Lab to better understand noise patterns in Rotterdam. You can shape the concrete research direction directly with Rotterdam, based on your interests and the municipality's needs.
    </p>
    <p><strong>Supervision:</strong> Lion Cassens (TPM, TU Delft)<br>
       <strong>In collaboration with:</strong> Municipality of Rotterdam</p>
