    <h4 id="broad-prosperity">Understanding people’s trade-offs in broad prosperity</h4>
    <p>
      Broad prosperity (<em>brede welvaart</em>) is increasingly central to policymaking, raising questions about how people value competing societal outcomes. This MSc project aims to understand people’s trade-offs across three pillars of broad prosperity: sustainability, inclusivity and justice. You will design stated choice experiments and analyse the resulting choice data, investigating non-linear preferences, non-compensatory behaviour—where improvements in one dimension cannot offset losses in another—and the decision rules underlying people’s choices. The findings will help inform policy appraisal beyond conventional economic indicators.
    </p>
    <p><strong>Requirement:</strong> Successful completion of SEN122A, <em>Statistical Analysis of Choice Behaviour</em>.</p>
