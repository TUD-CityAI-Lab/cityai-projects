    <h4 id="garbage-perception">Measuring the perceptual impact of urban waste placement: Bridging operational metrics and citizen-centric liveability</h4>
    <p>
      Urban waste placed beside collection points (<em>bijplaatsing</em>) degrades public space and creates operational challenges for Dutch municipalities. While the standard CROW condition scoring system objectively measures waste volumes, it fails to capture how residents subjectively perceive visual disorder. In this thesis, you will use a dataset provided by UrbanVue B.V. to design a paired-comparison choice experiment and apply Computer Vision-enriched Discrete Choice Models (CVDCM). By linking visual image features directly to citizen acceptability, you will bridge the gap between operational metrics and perceived liveability, yielding actionable insights for municipal waste management.
    </p>
