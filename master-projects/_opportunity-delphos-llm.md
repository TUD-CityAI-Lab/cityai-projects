    <h4 id="delphos-llm">LLM-Enhanced Delphos: Accelerating Reinforcement Learning for Choice Model Specification</h4>
    <p>
      Specifying discrete choice models traditionally requires extensive expert trial-and-error to balance statistical fit with behavioural plausibility. While <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18441" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Delphos</a> automates this process using reinforcement learning, training such agents demands high sample complexity and struggles with cross-dataset transfer. In this MSc thesis, drawing on recent advances in <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00282" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">LLM-enhanced reinforcement learning</a>, you will integrate Large Language Models into Delphos to act as reward interpreters and action translators for Apollo utility specifications. You will investigate how semantic prompting improves sample efficiency and facilitates transferring learned modelling strategies across diverse transport datasets. Ultimately, your research will advance sample-efficient, autonomous AI assistants for transport behaviour modelling.
    </p>
    <p><strong>Supervision:</strong> Gabriel Nova (TPM, TU Delft)</p>
